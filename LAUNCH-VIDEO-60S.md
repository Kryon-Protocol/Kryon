# KRYON — 60-Second Launch Video Script
Two founders, on camera. **A** = founder 1, **B** = founder 2.
Target: **152 spoken words**, ~2.5 words/sec. Do NOT add lines — 60s is unforgiving.

---

## THE SCRIPT

### [0:00 – 0:07] — Cold open · **A**
*[On screen: both founders, tight two-shot. No logo yet.]*

> **A:** Perpetual futures are the single biggest product in crypto.
> Nobody had built one on Stellar. **Today we did.**

*[Cut to logo card: KRYON — 0.5s punch. Then straight to product.]*

---

### [0:07 – 0:18] — The problem · **B**
*[On screen: a clean order book filling. Bids green, asks red, ticking.]*

> **B:** Every real exchange runs an **order book** — buyers matched to sellers.
> Blockchains are too slow for one. Stellar settles in five seconds; no trader waits five seconds.
> So everyone else dropped the book and just quoted you a price from a data feed.

---

### [0:18 – 0:26] — The insight · **A**
*[On screen: simple diagram — YOU → MATCHER (~1s) ← TRADER, arrow down to STELLAR.]*

> **A:** We kept the book. We **match off-chain in about a second**, and your money, your margin and your settlement stay **on-chain**.
> Exchange speed — without handing anyone your funds.

---

### [0:26 – 0:44] — Demo · **B** *(voice over screen recording)*

### [0:53 – 1:00] — Close · **B**, then both

> **A + B (together, plain, not shouty):** Come put in an order.

## DELIVERY NOTES

- **Hand-offs are the whole trick.** Each switch is a hard cut, no "and now my co-founder will…". Practice the four transitions more than the words.
- **Pause after "Today we did."** One full second. It's the only pause you can afford.
- **Don't sell during the demo.** B narrates what is happening, present tense, nothing else. If the recording has UI sound, drop the music.
- **"Powerless" is the word investors remember.** Land it. Slow down for that one sentence.
- Say "eight contracts, eight markets, live" as one breath — it's the credibility line.
- Never say "we're building." It's live. Present tense throughout.

## SHOOTING NOTES

- Shoot A and B on the same background, same framing, same day. Mismatched lighting reads as amateur faster than any script problem.
- Screen recording at 0:26 must be **real mainnet**, not a mock, and the tx hash must be legible for at least 2 seconds — screenshot-able is the point.
- Burn captions in. Most people watch this muted on a feed.
- Last frame: URL only, no music sting, holds 1.5s past the final word.

## IF YOU RUN LONG (you will, first take)

Cut in this order:
1. "Exchange speed — without handing anyone your funds." (0:18 block) — the diagram already says it.
2. "Blockchains are too slow for one." — the five-second line covers it.
3. **Never cut** the demo, "powerless," or the URL.
